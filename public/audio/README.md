# Forest background music

Place your calm forest music here as `forest-ambience.mp3`.

Open the live page and click the small music-note button in the lower-left corner of the forest. The track loops automatically at a gentle volume. The expandable panel controls both music and planting chimes and can be collapsed back to the single button. No music file is bundled; the chimes still work without it. Reload the live page after adding or replacing the file.
